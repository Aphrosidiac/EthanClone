Concept, campaign, and fashion editorial

I photograph editorial and fashion stories for brands, magazines, designers, and the creatives who build a whole world around a single garment. Every shoot begins with a concept and a mood, then loosens once we are on set, because the frames I love most tend to arrive in the space between the ones we planned. My eye is film-inspired and a little Y2K, with moody color, honest skin, and a softness that makes a picture feel remembered instead of staged.

Sometimes that is a full campaign with a crew and a call sheet. Sometimes it is one strange, beautiful cover image, or a lookbook that has to sell a season and still feel like art. Either way, I care about the story living underneath the styling.

Made for brands, magazines, and creative teams

On set I work like a documentarian who happens to love clothes. I read a room fast, direct without stiffening anyone up, and keep the energy loose enough that seasoned models, founders, and first-time talent all end up looking like themselves. I have shot for retail and hospitality names, product launches, and independent designers, and I am as at home running a full team as I am with one person against a bare wall and a single light.

You leave with a tight, considered edit and color that protects the film-inspired mood rather than sanding it flat, delivered in the crops and usage your campaign actually needs, from the hero image down to the frame your social team wants at nine on a Tuesday.

Washington DC and New York, and wherever the idea travels

Washington DC hands me marble, late light on federal stone, and a formality that is genuinely fun to break. New York hands me subway platforms, stairwells, and rooms that were never built to be pretty. I shoot fashion and editorial across both cities and travel wherever the concept wants to go.

If you are planning a campaign, a magazine story, or a portfolio that finally looks like the work in your head, tell me the idea. I will bring the light, the pacing, and the film-inspired eye to match it.
