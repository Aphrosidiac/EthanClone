    What happens next
    1. 01
      You send the inquiry

      One form, a couple of minutes. Your date, the kind of shoot, and what you're picturing. If you built a quote above, it travels with you, so you're not filling it in twice.

    2. 02
      We hop on a call

      Usually about half an hour. You walk me through the day, I answer whatever's still open, and we settle on what the coverage actually needs to be.

    3. 03
      Contract and invoice

      If it's a yes, I send the agreement and the first invoice together. Signing and paying the retainer is what secures the booking, and until that's done the date stays open to anyone.

    The promise

    What you see is what you pay

    When your inquiry arrives I fill in this same form from your answers. The quote you get back is this calculator's number, not a second one, so it matches what you saw here as long as what you entered matches the day.

    Five things are settled with you directly, because a form has no way to judge them.

    Travel
    Mileage inside the region is already in the number. A drive well past that is not.
    Gap hours
    The idle stretch between a ceremony and a reception is its own conversation.
    Intensity
    The cinematic ones, where the plan involves getting into the water.
    Aerial
    Drone coverage is priced on its own terms.
    The unusual
    Permits, difficult access, a venue that runs by its own rules.
