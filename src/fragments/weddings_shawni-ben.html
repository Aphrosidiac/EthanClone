Shawni & Ben

The DAR, Washington. June fifth. The day, as it happened.

Scroll
Chapter I

The Venue

Memorial Continental Hall, the DAR · Washington, DC

They both fell for it the moment they walked in, and knew this was the one. One block west of the White House, the Daughters of the American Revolution keep a Beaux-Arts landmark in white marble: thirteen columns along the portico, one for each of the original colonies, with a clear line of sight to the Washington Monument. On the fifth of June, it was Shawni and Ben’s.

Chapter II

The Styling

A welcome sign at the door, calla lilies by the armful, three tiers of cake, and tables set beneath the barrel vault. Timeless, elegant, the hall dressed for the evening.

Chapter III

The First Look

Ben waited at the balustrade with the Monument for company, nervous and unable to wait another second to see her. Shawni crossed the terrace behind him, and when he turned, he was stunned, wide-eyed, wow. The wedding party watched through the windows in beautiful awe.

He turned around…
Chapter IV

The Wedding Party

Laughter on the couch, bottles cracked around the table, and one very sharp lineup at the front door.

Chapter V

The Ceremony

A warm, bright summer day, and thanks to the hour it was called, the whole ceremony sat in cool shade while the sun stayed out beyond it. Under the flag, in front of everyone they love: the mothers walked, their nieces and nephews came down the aisle as ring bearers, the vows held steady, and the whole terrace let go at the kiss, the Monument behind it all, watching them marry.

Chapter VI

The Reception

Then the terrace turned into a dance floor. The DJ kept the whole place lifted, heart-shaped sunglasses came out, drinks flowed while the bartenders worked at the top of their game, and the wedding party stayed head over heels for the two of them all night. A crowd that would not sit down, and one last kiss on the balustrade.
